Priority: Medium
Status: Draft
Type: Smoke

# Preconditions

Kondisi yang harus dipenuhi sebelum pengujian.

- Players must have the items required to cook food
- Gather the items required according to the recipe

# Test Data

Data yang digunakan dalam pengujian.

- Recipe data

# Test Steps

Langkah-langkah yang dilakukan tester.

1. Enter Gameplay
2. Go to the kitchen
3. Open the cooking stove interface
4. See the recipe for corn porridge and find out what ingredients you’ll need
5. Close the cooking stove interface
6. Go to the storage
7. Open the stoge interface
8. Gather a few of the ingredients needed to make corm porridge
9. Close the storage interface
10. back to kitchen
11. Open the cooking stove interface
12. Choose a corn porridge recipe
13. Press the ‘cook’ button
14. Wait for it to cook
15. Once you’ve done that, close the cooking stove interface
16. And open the inventory
17. and look, the corn porridge is ready

# Expected Result

Hasil yang seharusnya terjadi apabila fitur berjalan sesuai spesifikasi.

- The ingredients required by the recipe can be cooked according to the recipe
- If it does not meet the requirements, it cannot be cooked

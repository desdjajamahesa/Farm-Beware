Test Case: Eating food and experiencing its effects

Priority: Medium
Status: Draft
Type: Smoke

# Preconditions

Kondisi yang harus dipenuhi sebelum pengujian.

- Must have food

# Test Data

Data yang digunakan dalam pengujian.

- Food
- Buff

# Test Steps

Langkah-langkah yang dilakukan tester.

1. Enter Gameplay
2. Go to the storage
3. open storage interface
4. Take the food from the storage to inventory
5. close the storage interface
6. open the inventory
7. Double-click to feed it
8. close inventory interface
9. observe the buff or effect below the health bar

# Expected Result

Hasil yang seharusnya terjadi apabila fitur berjalan sesuai spesifikasi.

- Food can be eaten by players
- Food provides a buff as specified in the document
- The food that has been eaten disappears

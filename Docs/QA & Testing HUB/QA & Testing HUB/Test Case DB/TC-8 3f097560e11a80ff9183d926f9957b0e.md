# TC-8

Test Case: Attack until defeat one normal enemy
Scenarios: Verify player can perform basic combat (../Test%20Scenarios%20DB/Verify%20player%20can%20perform%20basic%20combat%203ee97560e11a803695acdaf62c9acffb.md)
Priority: Medium
Status: Draft
Type: Smoke

# Preconditions

Kondisi yang harus dipenuhi sebelum pengujian.

- The player is alive, weapons can be used, and one normal enemy can be fought

# Test Data

Data yang digunakan dalam pengujian.

- Enemies,
- weapons,
- hit and damage rules

# Test Steps

Langkah-langkah yang dilakukan tester.

1. Enter the night phase
2. Get close to the enemy until you are within striking range.
3. Carry out an attack that hits the enemy.
4. Carry on until the enemy’s HP is depleted.

# Expected Result

Hasil yang seharusnya terjadi apabila fitur berjalan sesuai spesifikasi.

- Valid attacks are processed and the enemy’s HP is reduced in accordance with the damage rules.
- When HP is depleted, the enemy enters a dead state in accordance with the rules.
- The game continues to respond.

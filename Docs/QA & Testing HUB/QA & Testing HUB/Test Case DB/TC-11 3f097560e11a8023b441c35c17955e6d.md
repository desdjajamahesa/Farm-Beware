# TC-11

Test Case: Completing one day and night cycle
Scenarios: Verify day and night cycle progresses (../Test%20Scenarios%20DB/Verify%20day%20and%20night%20cycle%20progresses%203ee97560e11a80f19f2ae58407a370a3.md)
Priority: Medium
Status: Draft
Type: Smoke

# Preconditions

Kondisi yang harus dipenuhi sebelum pengujian.

- Enter gameplay
- Farming & Harvesting in the day
- Brawl with enemy in the night

# Test Data

Data yang digunakan dalam pengujian.

- Enemy
- Plant
- Item
- Drops
- Day & Night
- Combat

# Test Steps

Langkah-langkah yang dilakukan tester.

1. Enter gameplay
2. Farming and harvesting during the day
3. Go to Bedroom and sleep
4. Night phase coming
5. Brawl through the night until the enemy is wiped out
6. Go to Bedroom and Sleep
7. Day phase coming

# Expected Result

Hasil yang seharusnya terjadi apabila fitur berjalan sesuai spesifikasi.

- Day turns into night, the encounter is resolved, the players return home, and the next day begins as per the rules. There are no transitions that interrupt the flow of the game.

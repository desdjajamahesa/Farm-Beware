# TC-4

Test Case: From planting to harvesting a single crop
Scenarios: Verify basic farming activities are functional (../Test%20Scenarios%20DB/Verify%20basic%20farming%20activities%20are%20functional%203ee97560e11a8090ad89deb71da1d658.md)
Priority: Critical
Status: Draft
Type: Smoke

# Preconditions

Conditions that must be met prior to testing.

- Vacant plot
- one type of seed is available in sufficient quantities
- planting costs and yield are known

# Test Data

Data yang digunakan dalam pengujian.

- Type of seed
- planting costs
- growth duration
- yield
- water cost
- condition of the plot after harvest

# Test Steps

The steps carried out by the tester.

1. Take the seeds out of the box
2. hoed the soil
3. Plant in the plot.
4. Pour water over it
5. Wait until the plants are ready to be harvested
6. Harvest and check the stock and plots.

# Expected Result

The expected outcome if the feature operates in accordance with the specifications.

- Seed usage is reduced in line with planting costs. The correct crop emerges and matures.
- The harvest yields the expected output.
- The condition of the plot after harvest complies with the regulations.

# Notes

Additional notes regarding test cases.

- Once you’ve planted something, it needs water to grow, but there’s no water in your inventory; however, when you tap ‘water’, a bottle of water suddenly appears in your inventory.
